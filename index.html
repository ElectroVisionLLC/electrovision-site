See what your home actually costs to run.

ElectroVision turns a photo of a room into an appliance-by-appliance breakdown of your electricity bill — then shows you where the money is going.

How it works

  • Scan a room. Point your camera. Appliances are identified automatically.
  • Tell it how you live. Loads per week, hours per day, thermostat setpoint.
  • Calibrate against a real bill. Scan your utility bill or meter and the estimates anchor to your actual usage.
  • Track multiple properties. A home, an office, a rental — each with its own rooms and rates.

Built for accuracy, honest about estimates

Every figure in the app is editable. If the model guesses a wattage and you know better, change it — the whole projection updates. And because a scan-based estimate is only ever an estimate, ElectroVision lets you calibrate against a real utility bill so the numbers reflect your home rather than an average one.

Please read: these are estimates, not measurements

ElectroVision uses artificial intelligence to identify appliances and read meters and bills. AI can be confidently wrong. It may misidentify an appliance, invent a specification, or misread a number — and present it as fact.

Check anything the app tells you before you act on it. This is a tool for gathering and organising information about your energy use — not a professional energy audit. Don't make significant financial decisions based on its output without verifying the figures independently and speaking to a qualified professional.

Your photos stay on your device

Images are analyzed and discarded. They aren't stored on our servers, and bill scanning is explicitly built to read your kilowatt-hours while ignoring your name, address, and account number. Read the privacy policy.
