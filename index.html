What this is, and isn't

Built for honesty, not just hype.

Every figure is editable
Estimates use typical published appliance wattages and a $0.14/kWh rate. If a guess is wrong, correct it — the whole projection updates.
AI can be confidently wrong
It may misidentify an appliance or misread a number and still sound sure. This is a tool for organizing information about your energy use — not a professional energy audit.
Photos aren't stored
Appliance photos are analyzed and then discarded — never kept on our servers. Bill scanning reads only usage, ignoring your identity and account number.
