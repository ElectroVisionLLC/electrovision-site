The Unsplash Licence permits commercial use without attribution. The credit
above is kept anyway so the provenance of every shipped asset is recorded — if
a licence is ever questioned, the answer should be in the repository rather
than in someone's memory.

**Stock imagery that is not from a genuinely free source does not go in this
repository.** "Royalty-free" on Shutterstock, Getty and similar sites means "no
per-use royalty after you buy a licence", not "free" — their previews are not
licensed for use, and the agencies enforce this with reverse-image search.
Anything from those sources needs a purchased licence recorded in this table
before it ships.
